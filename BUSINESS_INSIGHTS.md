# Business Insights

## Airline Leadership

United Airlines is the dominant carrier, handling over 260 million passengers during the study period.

## Domestic Market Dominance

Domestic traffic represents approximately 76% of all passenger movement.

## International Growth Opportunities

Asia is the largest international market followed by Europe.

## Terminal Utilization

Terminal 3 handles the highest passenger volume and serves as the primary hub for United Airlines.

## COVID-19 Impact

Passenger traffic fell by more than 71% in 2020 but recovered significantly between 2021 and 2025.

## Strategic Recommendations

1. Increase investment in high-demand terminals.
2. Expand international route partnerships.
3. Focus on Asia-Pacific growth opportunities.
4. Improve operational resilience against future disruptions.
5. Optimize boarding areas with the highest traffic volumes.
