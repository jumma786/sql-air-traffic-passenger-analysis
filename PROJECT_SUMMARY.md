# Project Summary

The Air Traffic Passenger Analysis project investigates passenger movement across airlines, regions, terminals, and boarding areas using SQL Server.

The analysis covers more than 1.1 billion passenger records aggregated from 39,926 operational records spanning 1999 to 2026.

Major objectives included:

* Evaluating airline market share
* Understanding regional traffic patterns
* Analyzing domestic vs international demand
* Measuring terminal utilization
* Identifying long-term passenger growth trends
* Assessing the impact of COVID-19

The project demonstrates practical SQL skills frequently used in Data Analyst and Business Intelligence roles.
